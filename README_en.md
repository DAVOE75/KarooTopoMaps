## What is KarooTopoMaps

**KarooTopoMaps** is a premium cartographic suite and Android companion application designed specifically for cyclists. It replaces the default map style on your Hammerhead Karoo with **9 radically distinct, handcrafted map themes**.

Each style is calibrated for sunlight readability on Karoo transflective displays, maximizing singletrack definition, contour readability, and route contrast.

### ⚡ What's New in v1.3.0 (Major Release):

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Notice Regarding PRO Features**:
> The advanced features in the **PRO Custom Map Studio** are currently in **closed testing and validation phase (BETA)**. During this trial period, this section is temporarily inactive for the general public. We will soon announce the official public release so that anyone interested can acquire a lifetime license and unlock the complete studio. All 9 classic themes remain 100% free, untouched, and fully operational!

* 🎨 **Custom Map Studio (PRO)**: Design your own cartographic style from scratch or customize any baseline theme with complete visual control.
* <img src="art/ic_gis_presets.svg" width="16" height="16" align="center" /> **1-Touch Discipline Presets**:
  * 🚴 **Road Pro**: Ultra-clean high-contrast background, bold road network, and disabled contours/singletracks for maximum navigation clarity at high speed.
  * 🚵 **MTB Enduro**: High-precision elevation contours (10m/20m), high-visibility red singletracks, forest doubletracks, and active survival radars.
  * 🌲 **Gravel Pro**: Priority on dirt doubletracks, unpaved roads, and water sources; muted secondary roads.
  * 🌙 **AMOLED Dark**: Pure black background (`#000000`) for maximum battery life and zero night glare.
* <img src="art/ic_gis_sun.svg" width="16" height="16" align="center" /> **High-Glare Sunlight Booster**: Automatically boosts line widths by +25% and reinforces high-contrast outlines for pristine readability under direct noon sunlight.
* <img src="art/ic_gis_fountain.svg" width="16" height="16" align="center" /> **Survival Drinking Water Radar**: Elevates potable water sources (`drinking_water`) to top priority with an enlarged bright blue icon visible from high zoom levels.
* <img src="art/ic_gis_mtb.svg" width="16" height="16" align="center" /> **IMBA Trail Difficulty Grading**: Automatically colors singletracks according to official OpenStreetMap trail grades (Green = Easy, Blue = Moderate, Red = Difficult, Black = Expert).
* 👁️ **True Layer Visibility & Elimination**: Disable any element you don't need (highways, residential polygons, buildings, contours, etc.) so they are 100% invisible on the map.
* 📏 **Line Stroke & Pattern Customization**: Configurable line widths (Thin, Normal, Thick, Ultra) and dash patterns (Solid, Dashed, Dotted).
* 📐 **Cartographic Typography Controls**: Customize text scale (85%, 100%, 120%, 140%), style (Normal or Bold), and font families (Standard, Condensed, or Technical Monospace).
* <img src="art/ic_gis_export.svg" width="16" height="16" align="center" /> **Cloud / Code Profile Sharing**: Export your custom map style to a text code to share with riding buddies via WhatsApp or notes, and import instantly on any Karoo.
* 🗺️ **Native GIS Vector Symbology**: Clean technical gold-toned symbology based on GIS standards.
* 🔑 **Offline Cryptographic Activation**: Lifetime device license without relying on Google Play or internet access on the trail.
* 🔄 **Universal Dual Map Support**: Full compatibility with official Hammerhead maps and OpenAndroMaps (OAM).

## Theme Comparison

| Theme | Cartographic Identity | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **IGN** | Official 1:25,000 Spanish National Geographic Institute style (MTN25): crisp index and intermediate contour lines, detailed cadastral parcels, official waterways, and maximum outdoor readability under direct sun. | General exploration, hiking, mountain passes, and riding throughout Spain. |
| **Vintage Explorer** | 19th-century engraved monochrome sepia (copperplate lithograph / historic MTN50 sheet). 0% modern colors: your red route track pops with clarity. | Nostalgic gravel rides, bikepacking, and vintage events. |
| **Earth &amp; Gravel** | Organic alpine styling: reflective bone-white base, granular surface and track grade distinctions (grade 1 to 5: asphalt, compacted gravel, crunchy dirt tracks, doubletrack), and clay/sand contour shading. | Forest roads, remote gravel sectors, bikepacking, and surface transitions. |
| **High-Visibility DEM** | Digital elevation model with continuous hypsometric heatmap (green valleys to amber mid-mountain and terracotta summits) and black trails with white halo. | Mountain passes, elevation relief reading, and steep climbs. |
| **Trail Territory** | Dense structured pine forest green, high-visibility mulberry purple singletracks, and OSM technical trail ratings (S0-S5). | Enduro, All-Mountain, and technical woods riding. |
| **Granite Altiplano** | Scandinavian mineral minimalism: slate-gray monochrome base, ice-blue water bodies, and high-visibility official cycling networks (EuroVelo / ICN, NCN, RCN) over clean asphalt. | Pure alpine road, official cycleway touring, and clean geometry lovers. |
| **Tactical Dark Mode** | Matte anti-glare carbon black base without fluorescent accents. Off-white tracks with zero eye fatigue and reduced battery consumption. | Night rides, dawn patrol, and low-light winter workouts. |
| **Elevate MTB** | Classic central European alpine cartography (SwissTopo / Kompass style) featuring complete waymarking and traditional hiking trail colors. | High alpine treks and traditional mountain biking. |
| **ALT-MTB Special** | High optical contrast variant with saturated road casings designed for direct midday summer sunlight. | Midsummer blazing sun and harsh glare conditions. |

## App Installation

The quickest way to switch themes on the fly directly on your Karoo:

1. **Download the APK**: Download the latest [`KarooTopoMaps-v1.2.2.apk`](release/KarooTopoMaps-v1.2.2.apk) or grab it from [Releases](https://github.com/DAVOE75/KarooTopoMaps/releases).
2. **Install to your Karoo**:
   ```bash
   adb install -r KarooTopoMaps-v1.2.2.apk
   ```
3. **Launch KarooTopoMaps**: On your Karoo screen, open the app, tap **"Apply to Karoo"**, open the **Ride** app, and enjoy!

## Cartographic Origin and Credits

* **Official IGN Theme**: This style has been handcrafted and independently created by David García Pascual (hesiOX), modeled faithfully on the cartographic symbology, hypsometric colors, and contour line specifications of Spain's **MTN25 (1:25,000 scale) National Topographic Map** published by the **Instituto Geográfico Nacional de España (IGN / CNIG)**. It does not use, borrow, or copy code from Tobias Kuehn's Elevate theme, nor does it rely on external icon packs (`ele-res`). It is a pure, standalone vector Mapsforge rendering style engineered specifically for the Hammerhead Karoo.
